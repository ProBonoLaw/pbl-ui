**This policy covers personal data we handle as a controller.** It applies to personal data we collect and decide how to use as a business, such as the information of account holders, billing contacts, prospective customers, and visitors to our website.

**This policy does not cover the client and case materials that our attorney customers upload.** When an attorney customer uploads client files, case materials, communications, or other content to the Services, we process that content as a service provider and processor on the customer’s behalf, not as a controller. That processing is governed by the Master Terms of Service, the Data Processing Addendum at probono-law.com/dpa, and, for protected health information, the Business Associate Agreement at probono-law.com/baa. For that content, the attorney customer is the controller, and the customer’s own privacy notices and professional obligations govern. If you are an individual whose information was uploaded by an attorney customer, please contact that attorney to exercise your rights.

**We do not sell your personal data, we do not share it for cross-context behavioral or targeted advertising, and we do not use your personal data or customer content to train large language models.** These commitments are described further below and are consistent with the no-training commitment in our Master Terms of Service.

“Personal data” is information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked with you as an individual. The categories of personal data we collect or process as a controller include:

- **Account and contact information**, such as your name and email address, and, if you choose to provide them, your firm or organization name, telephone number, practice areas, attorney bar number, and profile photo.

- **Device and technical information**, such as your IP address, device identifiers, operating system, browser type, and language settings.

- **Usage information**, such as your interactions with the Services, feature usage, and log data.

- **Communications**, such as the content of emails, chats, support requests, and other messages you send to us.

- **Information we do not collect.** We do not collect your age, gender, or ethnicity, and we do not collect government-issued identification numbers or biometric information. The Services do not access or use your device camera or microphone.

**Sensitive data.** We do not collect sensitive personal data about account users as a controller. Sensitive client information contained in uploaded case materials is Customer Data governed by the Master Terms of Service, the DPA, and the BAA, not this policy.

**Statistical and de-identified data.** We may derive aggregated, statistical, or de-identified data from personal data. This data does not identify you, and we may use it as permitted by law. If we combine de-identified data with personal data so that it identifies you, we treat the combined data as personal data.
